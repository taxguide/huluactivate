# How to Activate Hulu via www.hulu.com/activate?

<img
  src="/images/login-button.png"
  alt="Activate Hulu Now"
  width="300"
/>

Activating Hulu on a Smart TV, Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, or gaming console is simple using **www.hulu.com/activate**. Open the Hulu app on your streaming device, obtain the activation code displayed on your screen, and enter it on the Hulu activation page using any web browser. Once verified, your device will be linked to your Hulu account so you can begin streaming instantly.

## Steps to Activate Hulu

Follow these steps to activate Hulu on your streaming device:

1. Open the **Hulu** app on your Smart TV or streaming device.
2. Select **Log In**.
3. Choose **Activate on a Computer** (if available).
4. Note the activation code displayed on your TV.
5. Open a web browser on your phone or computer.
6. Visit **www.hulu.com/activate**.
7. Sign in to your Hulu account.
8. Enter the activation code.
9. Click **Activate**.
10. Wait for your TV to refresh automatically and begin streaming.

## Troubleshooting Hulu Activation Issues

If Hulu won't activate, try these solutions:

- Make sure the activation code is entered correctly.
- Confirm that your streaming device is connected to the internet.
- Restart the Hulu app and generate a new activation code.
- Verify that your Hulu subscription is active.
- Update the Hulu app to the latest version.
- Restart your TV or streaming device and try again.

<Warning>
If your activation code expires, reopen the Hulu app to generate a new code before visiting **www.hulu.com/activate** again.
</Warning>

### Do I need a Hulu subscription?

Yes. An active Hulu subscription is required before your streaming device can be activated.
